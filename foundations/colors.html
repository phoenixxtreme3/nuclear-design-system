Color

One theme today. brand-primary is the single accent, with a small accent ramp for emphasis/subtle states; everything else is a neutral content/background/border ramp plus one inverse surface used by components like the header that need a dark treatment without a full dark theme. nav-* tokens are role aliases for the top bar and bottom tab bar.
