Foundations

Icons

The complete Nuclear v2.0 icon library exported from Figma — 64 glyphs across arrows, actions, status, communication, commerce, utility, media, the bottom tab bar, food tags, illustrated empty-states and the shop-type set used in the category filter. Single-tone glyphs are fill="currentColor", so they inherit whatever text color they sit in; multi-color families (food tags, illustrated icons) and the brand-colored cart-count badge keep their original Figma colors.

Library

Two icons — illustrated/discount-offer and illustrated/refer-earn — could not be exported at this pass; their SVG output exceeds the export tool's size limit even alone. Everything else Nuclear v2.0 ships on the Icons and Navigation pages is below.
