Navigation Header

The app's top bar — greeting, location, LoveLocal Plus badge, coin balance and search — rendered in two surface variants and reused, with small content swaps, across every top-level screen in the app.

Usage guidelines

Do keep the greeting to one short word ("Home"); use dark-background=true only on an actually dark section; truncate the address line rather than wrapping it.

Don't restyle the search field's radius or fill outside the token values; don't add more than one primary action to the status cluster.

Accessibility

  • The greeting is a real <button>, not a clickable <div>.
  • Search uses <form role="search"> with a visually-hidden <label>.
  • content-secondary on bg-surface-subtle is 5.2:1; content-on-color on bg-inverse is 15.9:1 — both pass WCAG AA.
  • Search and voice-search icon buttons carry an aria-label.
